## 📌 About The Project

Job hunting as a fresher is tough — most resumes get rejected by ATS (Applicant Tracking System) bots before a human even reads them. This tool solves that.

**ATS Resume Tracker** lets you paste your resume and any job description, then uses Claude AI to:
- Score how well your resume matches the job (out of 100)
- Show exactly which keywords you have and which are missing
- Give specific suggestions to improve your resume for that role
- Track all your job applications in one place

Built as a portfolio project to demonstrate real-world skills: API integration, UI/UX design, data persistence, and problem-solving.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Analysis** — Uses Claude AI (Anthropic) to deeply analyze resume vs job description
- 📊 **ATS Score** — Get a match score out of 100 with visual score ring
- 🔑 **Keyword Gap Analysis** — See matched keywords (green) and missing keywords (red) at a glance
- 💡 **Smart Suggestions** — 4 specific, actionable tips to improve your resume for each job
- 📋 **Application Tracker** — Log every job you apply to with company, role, date, and link
- 🔄 **Status Updates** — Track each application: Applied → Interview → Offer → Rejected
- 💾 **Auto-Save** — All data is saved automatically, nothing is lost on page refresh
- 📱 **Fully Responsive** — Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile

## 📸 How To Use

1. **Paste your resume** text in the left box
2. **Paste a job description** from LinkedIn / Naukri / Indeed in the right box
3. Click **"Analyze with AI"** and wait ~10 seconds
4. Review your **ATS Score**, matched/missing keywords, and suggestions
5. **Improve your resume** based on the feedback
6. Click **"Add to Application Tracker"** to log the job
7. Track your application status as it progresses
